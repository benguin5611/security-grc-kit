# Assertion of the management of Tanager Technologies Pty Ltd

> Worked example for a fictional company, Tanager Technologies Pty Ltd. Every name, figure, and date below is invented to show what a completed assertion looks like; replace all of it with your own organisation's real, current facts before use. Derived from [../management-assertion-template.md](../management-assertion-template.md).

We have prepared the accompanying description of the Tanager accounts-payable automation system (the description) for the period 1 October 2025 to 31 March 2026, based on the criteria set out in DC section 200, *2018 Description Criteria for a Description of a Service Organization's System in a SOC 2 Report*, in *AICPA Description Criteria* (the description criteria). The description covers the trust services criteria for Security, Availability, Confidentiality, and Processing Integrity set out in TSP section 100, *2017 Trust Services Criteria for Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy* (including the 2022 revised points of focus). The Privacy category is outside the scope of this examination; Tanager operates as a data processor on behalf of its customers and has not elected the Privacy category, and the reason is recorded in the description. The full description accompanies this assertion in the companion system description ([tanager-system-description.md](tanager-system-description.md)).

The description is intended to give report users information about the system that may be useful when they assess the risks arising from interacting with it throughout the period, in particular information about:

- the types of services Tanager provides, being business-to-business accounts-payable automation for mid-market finance teams;
- the components of the system used to provide those services, covering the infrastructure, software, people, procedures, and data set out in the description;
- the service commitments Tanager makes to its customers and the system requirements it has established to meet them; and
- the criteria used to evaluate whether the controls stated in the description were suitably designed and operated effectively throughout the period.

Tanager uses subservice organisations to deliver parts of its system. These are its cloud infrastructure provider, its workforce identity provider, its source-control platform, its observability platform, its incident and alerting platform, and its customer-support platform. The description covers only the controls of Tanager and does not extend to the controls of these subservice organisations; that is, Tanager has followed the carve-out method, and the subservice organisations section of the description identifies each one and the assurance Tanager relies on from it. The description also presents the types of complementary controls that management assumes those subservice organisations have implemented and operate effectively, together with the complementary user entity controls that management assumes Tanager's customers have implemented and operate effectively. The complementary subservice organisation controls and the complementary user entity controls are both necessary, alongside Tanager's own controls, to provide reasonable assurance that Tanager's service commitments and system requirements would be achieved.

We confirm, to the best of our knowledge and belief, that:

**a.** the description presents the Tanager accounts-payable automation system that was designed and implemented throughout the period 1 October 2025 to 31 March 2026 in accordance with the description criteria.

**b.** the controls stated in the description were suitably designed throughout the period 1 October 2025 to 31 March 2026 to provide reasonable assurance that Tanager's service commitments and system requirements would be achieved based on the applicable trust services criteria, if the controls operated effectively throughout that period, and if the subservice organisations and user entities applied the complementary controls assumed in the design of Tanager's controls throughout that period.

**c.** the controls stated in the description operated effectively throughout the period 1 October 2025 to 31 March 2026 to provide reasonable assurance that Tanager's service commitments and system requirements were achieved based on the applicable trust services criteria, if the complementary subservice organisation controls and the complementary user entity controls assumed in the design of Tanager's controls operated effectively throughout that period.

These assertions reflect the way Tanager runs its information security management system, which is certified to ISO/IEC 27001:2022 and drives the system requirements and controls covered here. We make this assertion in connection with the SOC 2 Type II examination performed by Ashworth Beale Assurance, Chartered Accountants, conducted under the attestation standards established by the AICPA and, for Tanager's international customers, under the International Standard on Assurance Engagements (ISAE) 3000.
